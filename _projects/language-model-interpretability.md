A research project focused on making large language models more interpretable and transparent. This work addresses the critical need for understanding how and why language models make their predictions.

## Research Goals

The primary objective is to develop methods that can provide meaningful explanations for model predictions while maintaining the high performance of state-of-the-art language models. This has important implications for deploying AI systems in high-stakes applications.

## Methodology

We introduce a novel attention visualization framework combined with gradient-based attribution methods to identify the most important tokens and attention patterns that contribute to model decisions. Our approach provides both local and global interpretability.

## Key Contributions

- **Attention Pattern Analysis**: Novel methods for understanding attention mechanisms
- **Gradient-based Attribution**: Improved techniques for identifying important inputs
- **Evaluation Framework**: Comprehensive benchmarks for interpretability methods
- **User Studies**: Validation of interpretability with domain experts

## Applications

This research has applications in healthcare, legal analysis, and educational AI systems where understanding model reasoning is crucial for trust and adoption.
