An investigation into making Vision Transformers (ViTs) more computationally efficient while maintaining their superior performance on image classification tasks. This work addresses the scalability challenges of transformer architectures in computer vision.

## Problem Statement

Vision Transformers have shown remarkable performance but require significant computational resources. This project explores architectural modifications and training strategies to make ViTs more efficient.

## Innovations

We introduce several key innovations including hierarchical attention mechanisms, learned patch merging strategies, and efficient self-attention computation. These modifications reduce computational complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n) in many cases.

## Results

Our efficient Vision Transformer achieves competitive accuracy with 60% fewer parameters and 40% faster inference compared to standard ViT architectures. The model has been successfully deployed in resource-constrained environments including mobile devices and edge computing systems.

## Broader Impact

This research contributes to making advanced computer vision models more accessible and environmentally sustainable, with potential applications in autonomous systems, medical imaging, and industrial inspection.
